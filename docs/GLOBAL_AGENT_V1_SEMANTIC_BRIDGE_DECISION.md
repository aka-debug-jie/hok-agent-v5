# Global Agent v1: semantic bridge decision

The frozen GlobalArena labels are not retreat-dominated, but the 23-session human video replay and
both mobile Shadow diagnostics collapse to `DISENGAGE/OWN_BASE`. ROI repair did not change the
result, and unlabeled consistency adaptation reduced simulator terminal performance. Device
transport, local geometry, threshold tuning, and another preprocessing variant are therefore not
the active repair surface.

The previous scenario-card proposal is retained only as a diagnostic contract. It does not teach
human timing, route choice, behavior distribution, or full-match strategy and cannot enter the
active training lineage.

The admitted successor is Human IfO Bridge v1. Human full-match videos provide temporal behavior
and state-transition distributions; GlobalArena provides exact macro transition labels; the frozen
DAgger policy prevents complete-episode regression. The sequence is shared temporal representation,
inverse macro dynamics, human-video pseudolabels, Human-BC, and optional transition imitation only
after Human-BC passes.

The authoritative stages, thresholds, stop conditions, storage rules, and phone boundary are in
`docs/HUMAN_IFO_V1_PROTOCOL.md`. No existing Shadow evidence, model, split, or failed lineage is
rewritten by this decision.
